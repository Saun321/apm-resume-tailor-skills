# Associate Product Manager, AI Support

Northstar Software is hiring an Associate Product Manager to improve an AI-assisted support product for small-business customers.

## Responsibilities

- Identify customer problems through interviews, product data, and support feedback.
- Translate insights into requirements and partner with Design and Engineering through launch.
- Define experiments and evaluate feature impact on activation, resolution rate, and customer satisfaction.
- Work with data scientists to improve LLM answer quality and product guardrails.

## Qualifications

- 1+ year of product management, product analytics, or adjacent experience.
- Strong SQL and analytical problem-solving skills.
- Experience shipping customer-facing products.
- Familiarity with generative AI evaluation is preferred.
- Comfortable operating with ambiguity and taking ownership.
