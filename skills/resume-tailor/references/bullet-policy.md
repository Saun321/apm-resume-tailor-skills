# Bullet Policy

## Evidence-shape Router

Start every achievement bullet with a concrete action verb; never start with After, When, Because, Within, Across, or Faced with. This action-first router uses one of these four routes only; outcome is supporting evidence, not the route.

- **Delivery-led** — use when a product change, capability, or formal deliverable is strongest: Action → change or deliverable → problem → result.
- **Decision-led** — use when a verified choice, prioritization, or tradeoff is distinctive: Decision verb → chosen path over alternative → triggering problem → result.
- **Collaboration-led** — use when influence, alignment, or shared execution across people is distinctive: Led, Partnered, or Aligned → collaborators → shared work or decision → effect.
- **Workstream-led** — use when a bounded PM function sits inside a broader PM- or team-owned initiative: Ran, Synthesized, Analyzed, or Produced → bounded workstream → artifact or decision enabled → result.

Classify by the distinctive evidence: use Decision-led for a tradeoff; otherwise use Collaboration-led only if removing people or alignment destroys the claim; otherwise use Workstream-led for a bounded workstream; else use Delivery-led. `Contributed` alone is not Collaboration. Route choice never upgrades ownership, delivery, metric, scope, causality, or technical depth.

Use a directional mix, not a hard quota. Delivery-led is normally the majority; Decision-led increases in judgment-heavy roles; Collaboration-led and Workstream-led increase in fundamentals-heavy roles. When verified evidence exists, retain at least one Decision-led bullet across an APM résumé; one to two is the normal target. Increase it for prioritization-, ambiguity-, analytical-problem-solving-, trade-off-, or technical-product-judgment-heavy roles. Never invent a choice or upgrade a team decision to meet the target. When verified evidence exists, retain at least one Collaboration-led and one Workstream-led bullet across the APM draft. Never force arithmetic or invent evidence.

Emphasis shifts and accepted patterns
-------------------------------------

The following public-safe accepted patterns teach structure and do not authorize evidence; uploaded or verified evidence controls claims.

- **Delivery-led:** `Launched **guided onboarding flow** from user interviews and competitive research, translating findings into prioritized requirements that improved new-user activation by 18%`
- **Decision-led:** `Prioritized **self-serve onboarding** over advanced customization after support data identified activation friction; focused first release on guided setup and improved activation by 18%`
- **Collaboration-led:** `Aligned Design, Engineering, and Go-to-Market partners on **shared launch criteria**, resolving ownership and dependency gaps to keep cross-functional beta on schedule`
- **Workstream-led:** `Ran customer interviews and funnel analysis for onboarding, synthesizing findings into **PRD and backlog recommendations** that guided product lead's first-release priorities`

Delivery-led emphasizes the named capability for a shipping-heavy JD, the solved user problem for a discovery-heavy JD, and remains action-first. Decision-led emphasizes the chosen path and rejected alternative; compress context before the tradeoff. Collaboration-led emphasizes who, how, and the shared effect; a collaborator list alone is insufficient. Workstream-led emphasizes the bounded PM function plus the artifact or decision enabled; a contribution verb alone does not prove collaboration.

## Ownership and wording

- Match the verb to actual ownership.
- Use JD vocabulary only when it accurately names existing work.
- Keep metrics attached to the action and scope that produced them.
- Never compress away a metric's measured object: name the capability, label, model, experiment, or business outcome behind every percentage or rate.
- Do not replace meaningful evidence with keyword density.
- Put the strongest screening evidence early when natural.

## Length

Draft toward 22–28 English words and treat 30 words as the maximum. Maximum 30 words. Omit articles only when meaning and natural grammar remain; keep hiring-manager context, and let the interview carry extra detail.

Target one to two rendered lines per achievement bullet and never exceed two lines when the chosen layout supports that constraint. If a bullet overflows, remove lower-value explanation, compress wording, or revert a weak tailoring change. Never shrink font size, margins, or spacing to force a fit.

## Bold

Use zero or one bold span per experience bullet. Bold only the key product decision, capability, framework, or formal deliverable—never the action verb, tool, team count, or metric. Keep the span short, usually 3–8 words, and place it early only when natural.

Use no inline bold in project or portfolio bullets unless the uploaded template already requires it. Read all bold spans alone: they should form a concrete, credible, non-repetitive product-evidence chain.

## Change log

Log only material choices: evidence addition/replacement, bullet split/merge/removal, ownership/scope/causality correction, structural trade, or unresolved Core evidence. Do not log routine keywords, grammar, punctuation, skills order, compression, or bold styling.
